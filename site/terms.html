1. What Xeet is

Xeet is a browser extension that detects token addresses and tickers on X (Twitter) and lets you request a token swap from that page. It is a non-custodial interface to third-party decentralised exchange aggregators — Jupiter on Solana and LI.FI on EVM chains.

Xeet is software, not a broker, exchange, bank, money transmitter, or financial adviser. We do not take custody of assets, do not match orders, do not operate a market, and do not execute trades for you.

Trading digital assets is high risk. You can lose some or all of the value involved. Risks include, without limitation:

  • Total loss. Token values can go to zero, including immediately after purchase.
  • Scam and malicious tokens. Anyone can create a token. Some cannot be sold ("honeypots") or are designed to steal value. Xeet surfaces third-party screening signals where available, but screening is not a guarantee and a token shown without warnings may still be malicious.
